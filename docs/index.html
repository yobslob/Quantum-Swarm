About

Quantum Swarm is based on Quantum-behaved Particle Swarm Optimization (QPSO). In every iteration it measures how spread out the swarm is (Swarm Diversity Index) and whether it is still improving (Improvement Rate). It increases the step size α only when the swarm is both clustered and stuck, and moves the worst particles when it stays stuck.

We compared it with PSO, Differential Evolution, Grey Wolf Optimizer and standard QPSO on 11 benchmark functions, with 30 runs per case.
